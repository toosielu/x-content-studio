# Format-specific editorial guidance

## Article promotion caption

Use this mode for a short post accompanying an Article card, including requests such as “写一段像截图这样的介绍”. The caption invites the reader into the Article; do not rewrite the entire Article or repeat its full title.

A useful shape is two short paragraphs:

- Start with the reader's concrete task, obstacle, or first action.
- State what the guide contains and what the reader can do with it. Mention prompts or diagrams only when they are actually included.

If using two paragraphs, give each a distinct contribution. After introducing the reader's task or decision in the first, use the second to add a supported reading benefit or scope; avoid summarizing the same steps twice. Honor the user's requested length and paragraph count, shortening within that range when material is thin. If meeting a hard minimum would require unsupported promises or repetitive padding, deliver the supported caption and identify the material gap and length shortfall outside it rather than silently violating the constraint.

Often 80–150 Chinese characters is sufficient, but follow the user's reference and requested length. Avoid forced questions, “建议收藏”, guaranteed outcomes, and unsupported personal experience. Provide one version by default.

## Monetization content

When the user wants content focused on making money, spend space on the paying customer, task, deliverable, pricing scope, and acquisition or delivery steps. Registration history and broad AI commentary belong only where they affect feasibility.

For each method, make these points discoverable without forcing identical headings:

- Who might pay and for which problem.
- What the creator must actually do, beyond asking an AI.
- What the customer receives and how it is checked.
- How a scoped service might be priced; distinguish trial examples from evidenced market prices.

Prompts should specify inputs, output, missing-data behavior, and a concrete acceptance check. Keep precautions adjacent to the step they affect rather than adding long generic warnings.

Separate cash income, customer refunds, avoided spending, and tool credits. Do not present tokens as cash, a source author's earnings as typical results, or a generated demo as a completed sale.

## X Article

Resolve the three-way image choice in SKILL.md before generating a complete Article, unless the request or a confirmed standing preference already supplies the answer. Pass that choice through writing and review handoffs so a text-only draft is not mistaken for the full illustrated deliverable.

Honor the selected title and requested level of detail. Use a concrete opening and useful section breaks. For a beginner guide, make the first action and final deliverable clear. For a concise Article, merge repeated background and retain the actual method, example, or prompt.

Preserve uncertainty and attribution when adapting sources. User-supplied text can be transformed, but a new cover does not make another author's article original. Do not silently remove attribution or pretend source experience belongs to the user.

## Requested cover and inline images

For X Articles, generate images after an affirmative image choice or a confirmed always-generate preference; an illustration's usefulness alone does not bypass the choice. For other formats, follow the user's visual request. Read [article-visuals.md](article-visuals.md) for the module-to-image mapping, prompt optimization with the available imagegen skill, and complete-set checks; drafting a caption alone does not require new images.

An affirmative Article or long-form image choice requires one separate image for every content module, plus a cover unless the user specifies another scope. Honor any user-specified count. Generate and deliver the image files, not just prompts or an illustration plan. Mark every module's insertion position and keep the set visually coherent. If generation is unavailable or fails, report the affected modules explicitly rather than describing a partial set as the completed illustrated package; preserve any user-requested draft-review checkpoint.

- For this user's Article covers, prefer 5:2 unless they specify another ratio. Verify final file dimensions, not just the prompt. Preserve existing image versions.
- Inventory source illustrations before promising to replace all of them. Deliver each replacement and a clear insertion order.
- Keep a coherent visual system and topic-relevant objects. When a recognizable product mascot or brand style is requested, inspect a reliable visual reference and use it consistently across every requested image. Never substitute a generic robot or invent an official mascot.
- Use readable Chinese labels and avoid unsupported earnings, reward quantities, prices, or performance claims in graphics.
- Inspect every result for missing text, incorrect characters, clipped content, and character consistency. Deliver usable files and a grouped download for a full set.
- Distinguish generated editorial artwork from actual product screenshots or official promotional assets.

If dimensions or text fail inspection, correct the specific defect and inspect again; do not silently deliver a known failure as complete. For dimensions, use a supported export or crop only when it preserves the requested composition; otherwise regenerate. For repeated generation defects, make at most two repair attempts per affected image, then report what remains unusable and deliver the usable results. Respect any user-specified retry limit. Do not replace requested Chinese text with English without permission.

When file export or grouped download is unavailable, provide the available native outputs in insertion order and state the missing delivery capability. Do not invent file paths, download links, exact dimensions, or successful inspection. Preserve earlier versions during repairs.
